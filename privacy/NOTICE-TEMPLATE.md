## Information we collect

Describe only what is actually collected by each enabled feature. Potential categories include:
- account and authentication data needed to provide the service;
- service usage and product analytics, if enabled and permitted;
- diagnostic, security and audit events needed to protect and operate the service;
- device/browser and network metadata that is technically transmitted;
- billing and transaction records processed by the selected payment provider;
- support messages voluntarily submitted by users.

Do not state that ORIN collects a category unless the live configuration and data map confirm it. Do not describe unconnected integrations as active.

## Analytics and Google

[If Google Analytics 4 is enabled, identify the exact Google Analytics property/service, the event categories and parameters collected, whether cookies or similar technologies are used, the consent choices offered, and the applicable Google disclosures.]

Optional analytics must remain off until the visitor affirmatively opts in where consent is required. Explain how to reject, change or withdraw a choice. State clearly whether denied users trigger any cookieless measurement requests. Do not claim “no data is sent” if advanced consent mode or another tag sends denied-state pings.

Google policy requires appropriate notice about the implementation and collected data, including whether it is linked to other data, and user consent or an opt-out opportunity as applicable. Never send data to Google that directly identifies a person or permanently identifies a device.
